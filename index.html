How to play

  • The house has 7 rooms. Each room has 3 glowing objects and one countdown.
  • Tap a glowing object to open its puzzle. Solve all 3 before time runs out to unlock the next room.
  • Every room is harder: less time and trickier puzzles. No puzzle repeats.
  • Each puzzle gives points (400 to 600) plus 5 points for every second left.
  • A wrong answer costs 5 seconds. If time hits zero, the house gets you.
  • Controls: mouse, touch, or keyboard (type answers, press Enter).
  • Escape all 7 rooms to win. Your best score is saved in this browser.
